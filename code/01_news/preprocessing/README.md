# MCST News Datasets

This repository contains two datasets of foreign-monitored news articles collected by the Korean Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (MCST) via the now.go.kr portal.

---

## MCST Full Dataset

Foreign-monitored news articles collected by MCST, covering both international (글로벌) and Korea-related (한국) coverage sourced from overseas media.

**Columns (12 total):**
- Article title (Korean & original language)
- Outlet
- Country
- Category
- Publication date/time
- URL
- Full body text
- Summary

**Number of articles:** 1,750,570

**Period:** February 19, 2015 – June 27, 2026

---

## MCST EconTech

Subset of the MCST Full Dataset filtered to the Economy (경제) and Technology (기술) sub-categories.

**Breakdown by category:**
- Economy: 216,901 articles
- Technology: 87,528 articles

**Breakdown by coverage type:**
- International coverage: 250,026 articles (82%)
- Korea-related coverage: 54,403 articles (18%)

**Schema:** Same 12-column schema as the full dataset.

**Number of articles:** 304,429

**Period:** February 19, 2015 – June 27, 2026

# NYT Datasets

This repository contains three datasets of New York Times articles collected via the NYT Archive API.

---

## NYT Full (Tech/Business/Science/Economy)

New York Times articles collected via the NYT Archive API, filtered to Technology, Business, Science, Economy, DealBook, Climate, and Health sections/desks.

**Columns:**
- Headline
- Abstract/snippet
- Publication date
- URL
- Section
- News desk
- Byline

**Notes:** English only. No full body text — abstracts only (~1–3 sentences).

**Number of articles:** 174,339

**Period:** February 2015 – June 27, 2026

---

## NYT Korea Subset

Subset of the NYT full dataset where the headline or abstract mentions Korea, Korean, Samsung, SK Hynix, Seoul, Hyundai, LG, Kakao, or Naver.

**Schema:** Same as the NYT full dataset.

**Number of articles:** 2,692

**Period:** February 2015 – June 27, 2026

---

## NYT Semiconductor Subset

Subset of the NYT full dataset where the headline or abstract mentions semiconductor, chip, DRAM, NAND, HBM, foundry, EUV, TSMC, ASML, Nvidia, Intel, Micron, export control, chip war, or AI chip.

**Schema:** Same as the NYT full dataset.

**Number of articles:** 4,635

**Period:** February 2015 – June 27, 2026
